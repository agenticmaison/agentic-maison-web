## Design Context

### Users
Hong Kong SME owners and operators evaluating Agentic Maison for AI transformation work. They need to understand the consultancy's work and reach a contact action.

### Brand Personality
Direct, restrained, concrete. Preserve Atelier Horologique, the locked Radley wordmark, and the live site's typography and brass palette.

### Aesthetic Direction
For the proposed /chateau route, Sean specifies a scroll-controlled cinematic tour through French Renaissance architecture with warm chandelier light, checkerboard marble and AI working rooms. The Matrix architectural references do not imply a green grade. This direction is scoped to /chateau pending explicit promotion approval.

### Design Principles
- Lead with AI consultancy identity and useful business outcomes.
- Use original brand assets and live tokens, never the placeholder v2 tokens.
- Keep meaningful copy and hero-screen interfaces in accessible HTML.
- Reserve readable copy space while keeping the important action away from extreme edges.
- Respect the staged review gates, reduced motion, skip navigation and separate portrait compositions.
