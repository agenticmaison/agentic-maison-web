# Chateau tour — editing guide

All copy, labels, the CTA text, the loader lines, the Telegram demonstration and the contact section live in `src/lib/chateau/content.ts`. Change a string there and it changes everywhere it appears, including the reduced-motion layout. Pacing lives in the same file: each scene has a list of `segments`, one per beat, giving the scroll distance in vh and the local frame range it covers. A segment whose `from` equals its `to` is a reading hold. To make a room linger, lengthen its hold; to slow a move, give its segment more vh; to shift a hold to a different moment, change the frame number. Copy timing is the `beat` on each scene, in local vh: `enter` is the fade-in range, `exit` the fade-out range. The tests in `src/lib/chateau/timeline.test.ts` check that the segments still sum to the approved 1,400 vh and that every scene reaches its last frame, so run `pnpm test` after a pacing edit.

Adding a room is one new entry in `scenes` and one frame folder under `public/assets/chateau/frames/`, with a poster under `posters/`; the timeline, loader and stills layout pick it up. The Sales projection is the only overlay pinned to the footage: its corners are the `plane.keys` on that scene, as fractions of the frame at given local frames, and it is shown only between the `visible` frames. If the Sales footage is ever replaced, re-read those corners from gridded frames before anything else. Never put copy, headings or UI into the frames themselves; the page owns every word.
